🎮 CASINO PLANET TUTORIAL

🏢 Managing Your Casino

Build games, decorations, and dining facilities from the lower toolbar to attract guests and earn passive chips. Click placed items to upgrade their capacity/income limits or sell them.

👥 Hiring Employee Staff

Open the Staff tab to hire dealers (boost game income), waitresses (speed up food delivery), security (catch pickpockets), scientists (generate research points), managers, tech support (repair machines), and entertainers.

🧪 Scientists & Lab Upgrades

Build Research Labs and hire Scientists. They generate Research Points (RP) over time. You also gain RP whenever you win a minigame! Use RP to unlock high-stakes games in the upgrades menu.

☀️ Day/Night & Daily Reports

Every 3 minutes, the "Day" ends. Guests are reset, and you get a financial report breaking down earnings/losses per table. Your progress is autosaved to browser storage at the end of each day!

🤝 Multiplayer & Co-op

Host a multiplayer lobby to generate a Room ID. Send it to friends so they can join and help you build, hire staff, or sit down together to play Blackjack and Craps at the same tables!
